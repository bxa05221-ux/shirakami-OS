# First-Visit T-Shirt

## Concept

A light-hearted Shirakami OS community item for first-time users.

### Front

**SHIRAKAMI OS**

### Back

> サーバールームに引きこもった中二病。
> それがAI。

Small line:

> Connect the Landscape.

## Positioning

This is a proposed community / first-visit bonus concept, not a product specification or a commitment to manufacture or distribute merchandise.

The phrase is intended as a humorous shorthand for the Shirakami OS observation that current AI interaction often exposes only a fraction of the capabilities available when AI is connected to a persistent human Landscape.

## Principle

The joke is not intended as a technical claim about AI cognition. It is a memorable entry point to the Landscape-centered philosophy:

> AI does not need to become human. It needs a meaningful connection to the human Landscape.
