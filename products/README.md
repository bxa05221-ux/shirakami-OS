## Why service artifacts exist

The Foundation describes the architecture.

The service artifacts show what that architecture can produce.

```text
Foundation
    ↓
Runtime
    ↓
Protocol
    ↓
Service Artifact
    ↓
Human Experience
    ↓
Observation / Evidence
    ↓
Architecture Revision
```

New artifacts should be added only when their role, protocol boundary, and relationship to Shirakami Runtime can be explained.

## Character / copyright policy

Core service artifacts should remain character-agnostic where possible.

Demonstrations involving copyrighted characters or fictional settings must be separated from the core protocol definition and handled according to applicable rights and usage conditions.
