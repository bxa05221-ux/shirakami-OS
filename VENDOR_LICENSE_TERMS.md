# Shirakami Vendor License Terms — Draft

> Draft only. Not a binding commercial agreement.

## Purpose

This document describes the intended structure for commercial vendor integration with Shirakami. It is a policy/design draft for later legal drafting.

## Vendor-neutral access

Commercial integration should be offered under published, vendor-neutral eligibility conditions. The intended model is equal access under equal conditions, not preferential licensing for a particular AI provider.

## Possible license scope

A final agreement may cover:

- protocol implementation and integration rights;
- API or hosted-service access;
- SDK or adapter distribution;
- conformance testing and certification;
- use of the Shirakami name in connection with a conformant implementation;
- audit/evidence interfaces required by the protocol;
- security and incident-reporting obligations.

## Semantic invariants

A vendor integration should preserve, where applicable:

- Human Gate as the final human authority boundary;
- non-propagation of execution, publication, merge, or decision authority through AI-generated context;
- Evidence identity and provenance;
- AIwitness observation boundaries;
- traceability requirements;
- separation between observation, analysis, proposal, approval, and execution.

## Modification

Vendors may implement the protocol in their own technology stack unless a final agreement expressly provides otherwise. The key restriction should concern conformance claims and preservation of normative protocol invariants, not an indiscriminate prohibition on implementation changes.

## Commercial terms

Pricing, support, service levels, liability, security requirements, data handling, termination, and jurisdiction are intentionally left for a future agreement reviewed by qualified counsel.

## No automatic exclusivity

The intended model does not grant exclusivity to a vendor merely because it integrates Shirakami.
