# Shirakami Protocol System Index v0.1

## Purpose

This index provides a navigable overview of the protocol artifacts currently integrated into Shirakami OS. It is an orientation and traceability document, not a claim that every protocol is executable or that every composition is semantically valid.


The sequence is a conceptual reading and development route. Runtime execution remains subject to explicit protocol boundaries, verification, and the Human Gate.

## Protocol Inventory

| Order | Protocol | Primary role | Boundary |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Anmon Layer | Surface hidden assumptions and questions | Does not decide for the human |
| 2 | 3D Eisenhower Matrix | Organize priorities across rotating dimensions | Does not establish objective truth |
| 3 | ThreadRPG | Render collaborative multi-voice dialogue | Does not replace human interpretation |
| 4 | Rensan | Connect and extend contextual chains | Does not authorize arbitrary semantic links |
| 5 | Kasen | Compose distributed contributions into a structured whole | Preserves attribution and distinction |
| 6 | Guide AI | Support navigation, reflection, and next-step selection | Does not become an autonomous authority |
| 7 | Manga Pipeline | Transform protocol content into reviewable visual documentation | Rendering is not verification of meaning |
| 8 | Radio | Produce staged conversational audio with editorial review | No autonomous broadcasting or publication |

## Shared Invariants

1. Landscape remains the primary context.
2. Evidence records observable transitions and limitations.
3. Protocols declare structure and boundaries rather than owning domain truth.
4. Runtime execution is separate from candidate generation.
5. Human review and final judgment remain explicit.
6. Uncertainty, provenance, privacy, consent, and rights must not be silently removed.
7. A rendered or generated artifact is not automatically verified.

## Verification Boundary

A protocol may be documented without being fully automated. Structural compatibility is not semantic compatibility. Candidate routes require inspection, and execution must pass the applicable human approval and verification stages.
