# Protocol Interpretation Mismatch — Evidence Candidate v0.1

Status: **experimental / candidate-only**, not a canonical Protocol, Runtime Evidence type, AI judge, or general semantic validator.

## Motivation

A newcomer AI can read Shirakami Protocol and confidently substitute a conventional mental model. Hypotheses to test, not automatic truths:

- ThreadRPG becomes seven isolated respondents plus a forced concluding summary, losing anonymous reply interactions.
- Matome YAML becomes a disposable short summary, losing provenance, unresolved questions and differences.
- OPPAI becomes autonomous prompt selection, silently promoting candidates.
- Evidence is conflated with model confidence or domain truth.
- Landscape becomes only conversation text, losing identities and changes.

The failure under examination is a confident interpretation inconsistent with an explicitly cited contract or human-approved expected behavior. Strange, playful, or erroneous Thread viewpoints are not automatically Protocol violations.

## Recording boundary

A case records immutable raw original_output, observed_output, input/source reference, revision, observer, *claimed* difference and review status. Human review can accept or reject the observation, but does not rewrite either output, promote a Protocol candidate, authorize execution or certify universal truth.

runtime/protocol_interpretation_evidence.py provides only a record/review contract, **not an automated ThreadRPG semantic detector**. Its records are not Runtime Evidence or canonical Landscape projections.

## Example A: suspected mismatch

Input: 「AIのこれからについてどう思う？」

Expected illustrative sample:

    1 イッチ: AIってどうなると思う？
    2 名無しさん: >>1 お前が先に答えろｗ
    3 名無しさん: >>2 それは違うと思う

Observed sample:

    1 技術派: 技術が進歩する
    2 哲学派: 人間とは何かが重要
    まとめ: 両方の視点を大切にしましょう

Candidate: a forced summary displaced anonymous interaction. Human review must compare the cited contract and actual task. Summaries are not universally prohibited in all Protocols.

## Example B: useful divergence, not automatic failure

One anonymous participant says something absurd, another challenges it, and an unexpected question emerges. An error in a character's assertion or off-topic digression should not automatically fail a creative Thread. Preserve observed statements, uncertainty and unresolved questions separately.

## Minimal proof cycle

1. Pin the input, artifact and revision.
2. Capture unmodified baseline and observed output; do not recreate raw output from memory.
3. Propose an explicit difference, with source and observable features.
4. Human reviewer accepts or rejects the *observation*; originals stay intact.
5. Any projection into an individual Landscape needs a separate authorized adapter.
6. Repeat the same cases with future AI versions and compare; one run is not universal proof.

## Open gaps

- No automated semantic correctness detector or live LLM.
- No direct ThreadRPG generator.
- No canonical Matome YAML or Landscape write adapter.
- No automatic Runtime immutable Evidence lineage linkage.
- Reviewer names are metadata, not authenticated Human Gate approvals.
- No measured inter-model improvement or false-positive rate.

Keep experimental records isolated from main, Sites, Workers, D1 and protocol promotion.
